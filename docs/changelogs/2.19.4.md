# RGX-Framework v2.19.4

Package: `RGX-Framework-v2.19.4.zip`, 110 runtime files, plus `release.json`.

- Timer-bar completion and cancellation callbacks are failure-isolated. An error cannot skip cleanup or prevent other consumers' bars from completing or being cancelled. Error reporting is isolated too.
- Natural expiry does not call the cancellation callback when no completion callback is registered.
- A callback may restart its own bar; cleanup of the previous run no longer hides the restarted bar.
